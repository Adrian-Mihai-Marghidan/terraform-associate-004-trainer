# Instructor guidance

The repository has no committed answer key for current learner attempts.
Before presenting an original scored set, verify and freeze its key and rationale under `private/assessments/`.
Do not expose the key before submission in timed mode.

The gentle baseline is unscored. Inspect the learner's reasoning and record help without treating these introductory observations as independent readiness evidence.
Ground explanations in the relevant official resource from [RESOURCES.md](../docs/RESOURCES.md).
Follow [ASSESSMENT_RULES.md](../docs/ASSESSMENT_RULES.md) for repeat detection, assisted work, invalid questions, and readiness criteria.
